A brief presentation

João Pedro Calado Barradas Branco Pavia received his PhD in Information Science and Technology in 2022 and is currently Assistant Professor in the Department of Digital Technologies of the School of Applied Digital Technologies at Iscte – University Institute of Lisbon.

He teaches courses related to computer networks, information security and artificial intelligence, at both the Lisbon and Sintra campuses.

He is a member of IEEE and of COST (European Cooperation in Science and Technology), where he collaborates with professionals from several countries on intelligent radio communications for seamless inclusive interactions. Another of his collaborations concerns physical-layer security solutions for trustworthy and resilient 6G systems.

He has been involved as local organizer and reviewer in several conferences and symposia, and is also a reviewer for several journals in information science and technology.

Research

  • Wireless communication networks. Beyond-5G and 6G systems: massive and ultra-massive MIMO, hybrid precoding and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS) in the sub-6 GHz, mmWave and THz bands, plus physical-layer security.
  • Cybersecurity. Automated vulnerability detection in web applications, penetration testing and intrusion detection for IoT, and the resilience of systems and networks.
  • Artificial intelligence and big data. Machine learning for financial risk prediction and computer vision for industrial inspection.
  • THz devices and sensing. Earlier work on frequency-selective surfaces, metamaterial filters and low-cost plasma detectors for imaging and structural health monitoring.
